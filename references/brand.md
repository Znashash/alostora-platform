# Alostora Brand Reference

- **Name:** الأسطورة (Alostora — "The Legend") · alostorajo.com
- **Sector:** Arabic animated-history e-learning (Jordan-based).
- **Logo:** open book + rising arrow / torch forming a checkmark → "knowledge that rises."
  Vector recreation: [`logo.svg`](logo.svg) (also at `theme/assets/images/logo.svg`).
- **Palette:** navy `#14315E`, blue `#2C6FBF`, orange `#F39019` / amber `#F5A623`, deep navy
  `#0A1428`. Full token list in [`../design/design-tokens.md`](../design/design-tokens.md).
- **Voice:** energetic, educational, youthful. Tagline: "التاريخ لم يعد يُقرأ... بل يُشاهد!"
  ("History is no longer read... it's watched!").
- **Direction:** RTL-first (Arabic), with Latin fallback for domain/numerals.

The torch/book motif recurs in the UI: hero, the "how we teach" step icons, and the accent divider.
